Your --env finish-args breaks the application: $PATH does not get expanded inside the sandbox & therefore it doesn’t find the executable anymore.

Other notes:

  1. You can’t use node there anyway, org.freedesktop.Sdk.Extension.node20 is an SDK extension not available in the Platform. finish-args are only relevant to run the app.
  2. Take a look at the actual example app, there’s a reason why electron apps use a base & zypak:
base: org.electronjs.Electron2.BaseApp
