It looks like you have a typo in your AppData.

It contains this 2 lines:

<release version="22.09" date="2021-09-20">
<release version="22.06" date="2022-06-24">

I think there should be 2022 instead of 2021 here. Because 22.06 has the newest Date, Flathub probably thinks 22.06 is the newest version.
