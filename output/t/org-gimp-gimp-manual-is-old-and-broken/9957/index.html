Trying to re-install the manual, `org.gimp.GIMP.Manual` (branch 2.10, the only one available (same as the one that removed)) is installed (as "pinned" = won't get removed by `flatpak uninstall --unused` command anymore) and GIMP v3 won't use that. Was this planned that the local manual is dropped completely and only online manual can be used with GIMP 3?
