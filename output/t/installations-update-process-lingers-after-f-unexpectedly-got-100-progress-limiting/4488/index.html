Problem description:

After invoking a:

flatpak update

or:

flatpak install PACKAGE_NAME_HERE

The update/installation process starts normally. Then it either hangs for about 30 minutes, upwards to several hours or it makes decent progress until the following error message appears:

F: Unexpectedly got > 100% progress, limiting

After this message the process hangs again for a very long time, often several hours. Worst of all, most updates/installations are unable to finish, with following error:

F: Calling system helper: CancelPull Warning: While pulling “PACKAGE_NAME_HERE” from remote flathub: Socket I/O timed out

After many retries they eventually finish, seemingly at random.

During updates/installations, when consulting the system monitor there is next to no internet traffic, despite the fact that flatpak is claiming otherwise. Also the reported download speed dips down into single Bytes/s, which is probably a measurement error by flatpak, but regardless, my PC runs now upwards to 12 hours and more, just to report that all updates failed.

Everything else on my system that is using internet, depending on country and servers, downloads at speeds upwards to 15 MBits/s, so i think my connection is fine. I’m in central Europe btw.
