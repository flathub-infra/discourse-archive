The most likely outcome is that the runtime will ship Mesa without encumbered codecs and there’ll be another extension mesa-full with codecs enabled that will be automatically installed unless vendor shipping Flathub excludes it. That being said, It’s still being discussed between Flathub and freedesktop-sdk teams.
