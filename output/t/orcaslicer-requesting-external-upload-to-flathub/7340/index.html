G-code generator for 3D printers (Bambu, Prusa, Voron, VzBot, RatRig, Creality, etc.) - SoftFever/OrcaSlicer

Orcaslicer is a 3d printer slicer that is a fork of Bambuslicer, which was a fork of Prusaslicer, which was a fork of Slic3r. It is officially licensed under the AGPLv3 (in lockstep with it’s forks) and has a official Linux appimage build.

Recently, I have finished merging an automatic CI build of the Flatpak. What’s left is some straggler bugs, like camera view not working. I would like to get the ball rolling on getting this into Flathub. The reason I am not making a PR against the Flathub repository is per Softfever’s request: they want to be able to automate it through their Github CI. As such I am wondering what the path forward for this situation.
