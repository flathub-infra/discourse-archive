KDE runtimes 5.15-21.08 and 6.2 released

Dec 6, 2021
B
Bartłomiej Piotrowski
Dec 6, 2021 #1

Hello everyone,

We would like to inform you that KDE runtimes 5.15-21.08 and 6.2 are now available on Flathub.

The 5.15-21.08 runtime ships KDE runtime 5.15 rebuilt on top of freedesktop-sdk 21.08. See its changelog for specific changes. The manifest of the runtime can be viewed at KDE GitLab.

The 6.2 runtime ships Qt 6.2, without KDE components, and is also based on freedesktop-sdk 21.08. The manifest of the runtime can be viewed here.

Apologies for the late announcement.
