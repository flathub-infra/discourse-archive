The armv7 support was dropped with Freedesktop SDK 20.08. On 19.08 it was supported until 2021:

GitLab

Decommissioning armv7 (#1105) · Issues · freedesktop-sdk / freedesktop-sdk ·...

This was decided in BoF at GUADEC 2019. See https://wiki.gnome.org/GUADEC/2019/Hackingdays/FreedesktopSdk/Notes We will still support armv7 until 2021 with 19.08 branch.
