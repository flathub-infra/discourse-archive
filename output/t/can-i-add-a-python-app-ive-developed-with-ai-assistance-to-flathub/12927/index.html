How much AI we can tolerate varies slightly between reviewers, but overall we see it as a liability (no matter how much people swear they review) for the quality, stability, safety, and continued maintenance over a long period of time.

We want to have your app, with your code, in, not the silicon valleys. Inclusion can be considered if there is honest work to limit it in the workflow to the absolute minimum and rework the tainted partd. But as long as your development and codebase rely on it, then it’s not super bueno
