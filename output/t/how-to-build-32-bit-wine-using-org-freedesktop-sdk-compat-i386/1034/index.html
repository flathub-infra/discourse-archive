org.freedesktop.Platform.Compat.i386 is not a runtime - only an extension for the runtime. You have to base your application on org.freedesktop.Platform/x86_64/20.08 and add the extensions via sdk-extensions (to make them available while building) & add-extensions (runtime).

You can take a look at flathub/com.valvesoftware.Steam as an example. You’ll need at least these extensions:

sdk-extensions:

  • org.freedesktop.Sdk.Compat.i386
  • org.freedesktop.Sdk.Extension.toolchain-i386

add-extensions:

  • org.freedesktop.Platform.Compat.i386
  • org.freedesktop.Platform.GL32

Also take a look at modules-32bit.yml on how to configure your modules.
