Hi.

I am the maintainer of ksensors5
https://sourceforge.net/projects/ksensors5/

Everything needed to compile for qt 5 and kde 5, and 6, and create flatpak packages for qt 5 and kde 5, and 6 is available.

I am asking if there is someone available to do the preliminary verification work and publication on flathub.
(obviously I am available to help and, if requested, take over the maintenance of the publication).

Translations are missing and not managed, for the moment I prefer not to have them rather than having partial ones.

It has been a long and hard journey to get here, and there is still a lot that can be done (with a lot of effort required and that I consider a priority), also and above all for this reason I ask for this help.
