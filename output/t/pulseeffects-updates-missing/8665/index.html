This only means the app is developed by a community under an open-source license, rather than being proprietary under one developer. For if an app is officially published on Flatpak, look for the verified checkmark under the app name.

For EasyEffects it is an verified app. So you could report this to the official issue tracker. Alternatively, you can also report it on the Flathub-Manifest repository.
