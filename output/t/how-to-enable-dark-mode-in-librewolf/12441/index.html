In LibreWolf’s settings, under “privacy and security,” disable resist fingerprinting. Then go to the “appearance” tab of LW settings, and you should have the option to set your theme to dark mode or to follow your system theme.

At that point Flathub should be able to detect that your browser prefers dark mode.
