Hi, welcome to the Flathub Discourse!

First of all, this forum is more about general discussion about Flathub and Flatpak. If you need support with an specific app, its recommended to use the applications help resources. If you look the app up on Flathub.org, you can find a “Report an issue” link for the app.

Secondly, just “app crashes all the time” is unfortunately not enough information.
You should provide information about the system (Flatpak version, is app up-to-date), your steps which causes the crash (is this after an specific action?) and, ideally, in the case of a crash a back trace (see the Flatpak section especially.
